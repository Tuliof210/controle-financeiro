# Elevation

Border-first, not shadow-first. A surface tier (`--color-surface`,
`--color-surface-raised`) plus a hairline is the default way to lift an
element, and surfaces are **flat** unless they genuinely float over content.

Shadows are cool and discreet — translucent ink, never decoration. The dark
theme casts the same geometry in black at a much higher alpha, because the
light theme's 3–12% ink is invisible against a dark surface.

## Deprecated — the hard offset shadows

The pixel-art skin cast a blur-less offset in `--color-border` to read as the
frame displaced. Those three tokens still resolve, and `@include
t.elevation(press | press-active | panel)` still compiles, only because five
`*.module.scss` files have not been migrated yet. They now alias the levels
above, so they no longer look extruded — that flattening is expected, not a
regression. **Nothing new may use them.**

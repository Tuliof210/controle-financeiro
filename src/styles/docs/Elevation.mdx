# Elevation

Border-first, not shadow-first. A surface tier (`--color-surface`,
`--color-surface-raised`) plus a hairline is the default way to lift an
element, and surfaces are **flat** unless they genuinely float over content.

Shadows are cool and discreet — translucent ink, never decoration. The dark
theme casts the same geometry in black at a much higher alpha, because the
light theme's 3–12% ink is invisible against a dark surface.

## The hard offset shadows are gone

The pixel-art skin cast a blur-less offset in `--color-border` so a control read
as its own frame displaced, and paired it with `transform: translate(2px, 2px)`
on press. Both halves are removed: the three offset tokens no longer exist, and
the mixin has no branch for them either — asking for one raises a Sass `@error`,
so a stale call site fails the build instead of silently aliasing something
else. The four levels above are the whole scale.

A control's press is now `transform: scale(0.985)` — no displacement, and no
shadow needed to justify one.

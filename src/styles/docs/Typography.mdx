# Typography

Three faces, one job each. `--font-display` (Clash Display, weight 600) is
heros and titles. `--font-sans` (Hanken Grotesk) is body copy, labels and
**every money figure**, always with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.
`--font-mono` (IBM Plex Mono) is scoped to uppercase eyebrows, IDs, `YYYY-MM`
dates, hex, and chart axis/tag labels — never body copy.

`text-transform: uppercase` and `--tracking-wide` travel with that eyebrow case
and nowhere else. The wide tracking existed to open up a bitmap face with no
sidebearing; on a grotesque it reads as shouting. A status badge, a table
header and a checkbox label are sentence case.

# Radius & Borders

Corners are soft, never square and never a capsule: `--radius-sm` (6) for chips
and inner inputs, `--radius-md` (10) for buttons and fields, `--radius-lg` (14)
for product cards, `--radius-xl` (20) for large surfaces and sheets.
`--radius-full` exists only for avatars and status dots, never for a
rectangular surface.

Borders are hairlines, and they carry separation **before** a shadow does.
`--color-border` is the default, `--color-border-subtle` the quietest split,
`--color-border-strong` the one that has to survive a busy surface.

## Shape carries projection state

real / estimated / simulated must be distinguishable without color: a solid
hairline is real, dotted is estimated, dashed cobalt is simulated. The label
always comes with it.

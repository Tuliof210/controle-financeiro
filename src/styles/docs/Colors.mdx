# Colors

Neutrals carry ~90% of the interface. Cobalt is the only brand hue and is spent
with restraint — the primary action, focus, and the mark. Semantic tokens are
the ones components consume; never reach for a primitive directly.

## Neutrals (theme-independent primitives)

<Grid names={neutrals} />

## Cobalt — the single brand ramp

`--cobalt-600` is the anchor. Cobalt is a **themed** fill — `--cobalt-600` in
light, `--cobalt-400` in dark — so the ink on it is `--color-bg`, which flips
with it, exactly like the four semantic fills below. White holds only in the
light theme: on dark cobalt it measures 3.64:1 at rest and 2.25:1 on hover.

<Grid names={cobalt} />

## Semantic trios (theme-aware)

Financial state only: a normal expense is **not** red. Each foreground pairs
with its own wash and hairline, and every pair clears 4.5:1 in both themes.
Three of the foregrounds sit one step darker than the source design system —
`#0e8c57`, `#b5560e` and `#0f73a6` measured 3.88, 4.43 and 4.47:1 on their own
washes, under rule 7's floor.

<Grid names={trios} />

## Category hues

One per `PALETTE` key in `src/lib/palette.ts`. The **keys** are persisted
strings validated by a `z.enum`, so they never change name — only the value
moved. Each hue is distinguishable from the others and from cobalt.

## Navigation rail

The rail has no palette of its own. It was dark chrome in both themes and
needed one; it is now a light surface with a hairline to its right, which is
`--color-surface` and `--color-border` — so the five `--rail-*` aliases were
deleted rather than kept as a second name for tokens that already exist. The
active item is a 12% cobalt wash over the surface with `--color-brand-text` on
it, not a solid brand fill: cobalt is spent on the primary action, focus and
the mark, and a filled rail item competes with the real action on the page.

## Gradients

The background is flat: no decorative gradients, no glassmorphism, no texture.
`--glow-brand` is the only gradient in the system — a cobalt radial glow on a
dark hero, never on the mark. `--gradient-toxic` is deleted: the ramp swap
turned its lime→violet into cobalt→cobalt, and its one consumer paints
`--color-brand` flat now.

An overlay scrim is one translucent ink rather than a tint of the page —
`--neutral-950` at 55%, the same in both themes, because a scrim that flips
lightens the very page it is meant to push back.
